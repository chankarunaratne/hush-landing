Privacy Policy for Hush

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Hush is a Chrome extension that provides a clean, distraction-free reader mode for web articles. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. What data we collect

Hush processes the following information:

  • Article content from the web pages you visit (title, body text, author, and metadata).
  • Your system theme preference (light/dark mode), stored locally on your browser.

2. How we use this data

To display a simplified reading view of the article.

3. What data we store

  • We do not store any personal data on our servers.
  • All settings (e.g., theme preference) are stored locally in your browser using localStorage.

4. Data sharing

Hush does not share any user data with third parties.

5. Third-party services

Hush does not use any third-party analytics, trackers, or advertising services.

6. Your control

  • Hush runs only when you activate it. It does not auto-run or collect data in the background.
  • You may disable or remove the extension at any time via Chrome's extension settings.
